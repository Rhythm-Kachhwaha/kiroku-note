### 2.2 Reference Material vs Question Material

Non-exercise reference lists discovered on the site are explicitly classified and filtered:
- `https://japanesetest4you.com/jlpt-n1-grammar-list/` through `jlpt-n5-grammar-list/`
- `https://japanesetest4you.com/jlpt-n1-vocabulary-list/` through `jlpt-n5-vocabulary-list/`
- Filter Rule: Discarded by `url_filter.py` with pattern `r"/jlpt-n\d+-(?:grammar|vocabulary|kanji)-list/"` so they are never mistakenly crawled as question exercises.

### 2.3 Non-Question Content Excluded
The crawler and URL filter deterministically reject:
- **eBooks:** `https://japanesetest4you.com/ebook-*/` (e.g., Grammar & Vocab PDFs).
- **Advertisements & Monetization:** Google AdSense wrappers, Patreon links, and Amazon affiliate banners.
- **Otaku Corner / Blog:** Unrelated anime quote blog posts and non-JLPT cultural articles.

## 3. Discovered Question Formats & Official 20-Format Mapping

Every distinct exercise structure across N1–N5 was inspected, analyzed, and mapped to Kiroku's M2 Question Families (F1–F6) and the official 20 JLPT format registry (`0002_seed_formats_and_exams.sql`):

| Discovered Format | Observed JLPT Levels | Structural Characteristics | Official Registry Code | Question Family |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grammar Selection** | N5, N4, N3, N2, N1 | Sentence stem with blank (`_______`), 4 grammar choices | `sentential_grammar_1` | **F1** |
| **Star Sentence Ordering** | N5, N4, N3, N2, N1 | Sentence with 4 blanks (`＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿`), 4 phrase fragments, star derivation | `sentence_composition` | **F2** |

| **Reading Comprehension (Short/Mid)** | N5, N4, N3, N2, N1 | Reading passage (100–600 words) with 1–3 associated questions | `comprehension_short` / `comprehension_mid` | **F3** |

| **Listening with Shared Context** | N2, N1 | Shared passage audio introducing a scenario, followed by subquestions | `integrated_listening` | **F6** |

### 3.1 Star Sentence Ordering (F2) Structure Preservation
Star questions contain essential structural information that must not be flattened into plain text:
- The parser extracts:
  - `star_position`: 1-based index of the star (e.g., position 3 in `＿＿ ＿＿ ★ ＿＿`).
  - `blanks_count`: Total blank positions (typically 4).
  - `pre_context`: Sentence text preceding the blanks.
  - `post_context`: Sentence text succeeding the blanks.
  - `fragments`: Ordered list of the 4 choice fragments.
  - `derived_star_fragment`: When the answer key specifies choice 2 (fragment "食べた"), the star fragment is deterministically bound.
- Stored directly in `StarArrangementMetadata` on `ParsedQuestion`.

### 3.2 Kanji Multi-Question Context Handling
In Kanji tests, questions often share a single contextual sentence or present a 5-line block where line 0 is the target word (e.g. `1. 外国\n1. がいこく\n...`):
- The parser identifies 5-line blocks, strips the target word from the choice list, prepends it to the stem context (`【外国】`), and leaves exactly 4 clean choices.
- Prevents choice count corruption and preserves 100% format validity.

### 3.3 Strict Ambiguity Diagnostics (No Misclassification)
Rather than guessing or defaulting when encountering malformed or non-exercise HTML:
- The parser validates choice bounds (must have 3 or 4 choices for F1–F6).
- If choices are absent (e.g., non-exercise informational post) or invalid (e.g., 2 choices or 6 choices), the question is flagged:
  - `is_supported = False`
  - `unsupported_reason = "Invalid choice count: expected 3 or 4, found X"`
- The payload surfaces this to the ingestion boundary, completely preventing corrupted records from entering review.

## 4. Actual Audio Retrieval & Media Handling

Listening support was verified through direct inspection of the live WordPress audio players and network responses.

### 4.1 Audio Player Embedding Architecture
On JapaneseTest4You listening pages, audio players are embedded natively via WordPress block audio tags:
```html
<figure class="wp-block-audio">
  <audio controls="" src="https://japanesetest4you.com/choukai/n5/l5-1.mp3"></audio>
</figure>
```
Key observations:
1. **Direct MP3 Source:** Audio tracks are not behind blobs, HLS streams, or dynamic JavaScript players. They are direct HTTPS links to static MP3 files hosted at `https://japanesetest4you.com/choukai/{level}/{track}.mp3`.
2. **Zero Authentication/Cookies:** No tokens, sessions, cookies, or DRM are required to stream or download.
3. **HTTP 200 & Proper MIME Types:** Server responds with standard `Content-Type: audio/mpeg` and `Accept-Ranges: bytes`.

### 4.2 Playable Binary Verification
Live downloads were inspected at binary level:
- Downloaded audio headers start with `ID3v2.3.0` (`49 44 33 03 00 ...`).
- Raw MP3 streams follow with standard MPEG frame sync bits (`0xFFFB`, `0xFFFA`, `0xFFF3`, `0xFFF2`).
- The files play cleanly and independently on native Windows media decoders (`Windows Media Player`, VLC, ffmpeg) without network connectivity.

### 4.3 Integration with Content-Addressed MediaStore
The `AssetDownloader` was extended to retrieve and validate audio files seamlessly alongside images:
- **Streaming Ingestion:** Downloads stream directly into a temporary file, calculating SHA-256 on the fly.
- **MIME & Binary Validation:** Validates `is_valid_audio_bytes()` before moving into content-addressed storage.
- **Content-Addressed Path:** Saved to `media/<sha[:2]>/<sha>.mp3`.
- **Zero-DB Mode:** Operates safely without a database connection (`conn=None`), returning `asset_id` and `local_path` for candidate linking.
- **Audio Scoping:** Preserves exact relationship (`question` scope, `passage` scope, or `shared` scope) via the `AudioAssociation` data model.

## 5. Preservation of Text Fidelity & Source Claims

1. **Japanese Character Integrity:**
   - Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana, Japanese punctuation (`。`, `、`, `「`, `」`, `〜`), and furigana markup are preserved bit-for-bit without lossy ASCII conversions.
2. **HTML & Markdown Flexibility:**
   - Handled arbitrary nesting of `<p>`, `<strong>`, `<b>`, `<span>`, `<div>`, and `<figure>` tags.
   - Handled both Markdown-style lists (`1. choice`) and HTML radio choice lists (`<input type="radio"> choice`).
3. **Answer Key Rationale Preservation:**
   - Discovered 6 distinct rationale formats across exercises:
     1. Standard inline: `1. A - Explanation`
     2. Bold labeled: `1. **Answer:** B (explanation)`
     3. Separated dash: `1 - C`
     4. Parenthesized: `1. (D) Explanation`
     5. Arrow format: `1 -> A`
     6. Bracketed: `[1] B`
   - All 6 patterns are parsed into structured `source_answer_claim` records preserving the original claim text and rationale.
