〜のに JLPT N3
Core Meaning

even though; despite; although (expresses speaker dissatisfaction, unexpected outcome, or frustration)

Formation Rules
Verb (plain) + のに · い-adj + のに · な-adj + なのに · Noun + なのに
Example Sentence

約束したのに、彼は来なかった。

Even though he promised, he didn't come.

〜になる JLPT N5
Core Meaning

to become; to turn into; to change into

Formation Rules
Noun + になる · な-adj + になる · い-adj (く) + なる

When spring comes, it becomes warm.
