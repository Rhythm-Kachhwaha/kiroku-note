〜のに JLPT N3
Conjunctive Particle · 逆接助詞
Core Meaning

even though; despite; although Distinct nuance: conveys the speaker's emotional frustration, mild complaint, or disappointment when an outcome defies reasonable expectation.

Formation Rules
Verb (plain) + のに 行くのに / 食べたのに
い-Adjective + のに 高いのに / 寒かったのに
な-Adjective + なのに 好きなのに / 静かなのに
Noun + なのに 日曜日なのに / 雨なのに
Authentic Literary Exemplar

約束したのに、彼は時間になっても来なかった。

"Even though he had promised, he did not show up even when the time had come."
