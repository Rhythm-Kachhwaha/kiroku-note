==============================================================================
Kiroku Note — Browser Extension Setup Instructions
==============================================================================

Kiroku Note's Chromium extension is installed locally at:
  C:\Program Files\Kiroku Note\extension\
  (or your chosen custom installation directory)

To load the extension into Brave, Chrome, Edge, or Chromium:

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   - Brave:    brave://extensions
   - Chrome:   chrome://extensions
   - Edge:     edge://extensions

2. Enable "Developer mode" using the toggle switch (usually in the top-right corner).

3. Click the "Load unpacked" button.

4. Browse to and select the extension directory:
   C:\Program Files\Kiroku Note\extension

5. The "Kiroku Note" extension will now appear in your browser extensions list!

6. Pin the extension icon to your browser toolbar for quick one-click access.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Application Notes:
- Start the Kiroku Note desktop application from the Start Menu.
- The local backend service runs on http://127.0.0.1:21828.
- Your mined vocabulary cards, database, and media are safely stored in:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\KirokuNote\
  and will never be deleted by application upgrades or uninstalls.
